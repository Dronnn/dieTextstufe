Privacy policy

Your text stays on your device.

Effective: 10 November 2026

Textstufe estimates the reading difficulty of German texts. It is designed to work without an account, a server, analytics, advertising, or a shared text database.

What the app processes

Text you enter is analysed in memory on your device using Apple’s NaturalLanguage framework and a bundled scoring model. The app does not save your text permanently, does not build a history, and does not send the text to the developer or any third party.

What the app does not collect

Textstufe does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics or telemetry, no advertising or tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data, and no cloud processing.

Clipboard

The result screen offers a “Copy result” action. It places a summary on the system clipboard only when you tap it. The app never reads the clipboard.

Donations

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store’s in-app purchase system. Apple handles payment details, receipts, and purchase records under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your name, email address, or payment card details. Apple may provide the developer with aggregate sales and tax information.

Textstufe asks the App Store for localized product prices so the support screen can display the price for your storefront. This request is handled by StoreKit. Donations are optional, consumable tips and do not unlock features.

Data deletion

The app stores no text history and no account. Uninstalling Textstufe removes the app and its local data from your device. The developer has nothing to delete.

Children

Textstufe is a general-audience educational utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be published at this URL with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or support requests: Textstufe support or GitHub Issues.
