Deutsches Textniveau

How hard is this German text?

Paste a passage. Textstufe estimates its reading difficulty, shows an approximate CEFR level and a ZIX-compatible understandability score. Everything runs on your device.

Honest by design

Short inputs get no grade. A single word like “Hallo” never becomes C2. Results near a level boundary show the neighbouring level too.

ZIX-compatible

The six ZIX features and trained coefficients, ported to Swift. Model data and vocabularies are MIT-licensed and attributed in the app.

Private and offline

No account, no analytics, no upload. Your text is analysed in memory and never leaves the device.

Twelve languages

The interface follows your device language: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Japanese, Chinese, Portuguese, Turkish.

Optional tips

Textstufe is free. Six optional consumable tips support development and unlock nothing.

Made by one person

Designed, built and maintained independently. No subscription, no advertising, no dark patterns.
